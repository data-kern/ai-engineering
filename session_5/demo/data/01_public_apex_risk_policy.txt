INVESTMENT RISK POLICY & TRADING GUIDELINES 2026
PUBLIC INTERNAL POLICY DOCUMENT (ACCESS TIER 1 - ALL ADVISORS)

========================================================
SECTION 1: CLIENT RISK PROFILES & TAXONOMY
========================================================

Apex Financial classifies every client account into one of three distinct risk profiles:
  - Conservative (Risk Tier C): Focus on capital preservation and low drawdown.
  - Moderate (Risk Tier M): Balanced approach seeking moderate growth with controlled volatility.
  - Aggressive (Risk Tier A): Maximizing capital appreciation, accepting high drawdown.

Risk categories are assigned during formal onboarding through risk-tolerance assessments.
No financial advisor may modify or override a client's risk profile without explicit written
approval from the Chief Compliance Officer.

========================================================
SECTION 2: CONSERVATIVE CLIENT MANDATES (TIER C)
========================================================

2.1 MAXIMUM EQUITY EXPOSURE
Conservative clients must not hold more than 30% of total portfolio value in equity
instruments of any category. This ceiling encompasses common equities, individual shares,
equity index mutual funds, and equity Exchange-Traded Funds (ETFs). The remaining 70% must
be allocated to fixed income, money market instruments, or cash equivalents.

2.2 SINGLE-STOCK CONCENTRATION LIMIT
No single equity holding may represent more than 5% of total portfolio valuation for any
Conservative client account. Diversification is strictly mandated.

2.3 VOLATILITY & BETA CEILING
Advisors are strictly prohibited from recommending or executing trades in equities possessing
a 12-month trailing beta exceeding 1.5 relative to the S&P 500 benchmark. High-volatility equities,
leveraged exchange-traded products, and speculative micro-caps are prohibited.

2.4 PERMITTED INSTRUMENTS
Permitted asset classes for Conservative clients comprise:
  - Sovereign Government Bonds (rated AAA or AA)
  - Investment-grade Corporate Fixed Income (rated BBB+ or superior)
  - Broad diversified index mutual funds (tracking S&P 500, MSCI World)
  - High-dividend blue-chip defensive equities with beta below 0.8
  - Treasury bills, term deposits, and money market cash reserves

2.5 PROHIBITED ADVISORY ACTIONS
The following actions constitute direct policy violations:
  - Concentrated equity positions in high-multiple technology or growth sectors
  - Derivative options trading, short selling, or margin borrowing
  - Recommending asset allocations where total equity exceeds 30%

========================================================
SECTION 3: MODERATE CLIENT MANDATES (TIER M)
========================================================

3.1 MAXIMUM EQUITY ALLOCATION
Moderate clients may maintain up to 60% of total assets under management in equity instruments.

3.2 SINGLE-STOCK CONCENTRATION LIMIT
No single individual equity holding may exceed 10% of total portfolio valuation.

3.3 VOLATILITY THRESHOLDS
Moderate clients may hold securities with trailing beta up to 2.0, provided that aggregate exposure
to securities with beta greater than 1.5 does not exceed 20% of aggregate portfolio value.

========================================================
SECTION 4: AGGRESSIVE TRADING MANDATES (TIER A)
========================================================

4.1 MAXIMUM EQUITY ALLOCATION
Aggressive clients may allocate up to 90% of total portfolio capital to equities and equity derivatives.

4.2 AGGRESSIVE TRADING LIMITS & CONCENTRATION
Under Section 4.2 of the Trading Handbook:
  - Single-stock concentration limit: No individual equity position may exceed 20% of total portfolio value.
  - Sector concentration limit: Total exposure to a single industry sector is capped at 40%.
  - Leverage limit: Net effective leverage via margin or structured notes must not exceed 1.5x total NAV.
Advisors managing Aggressive accounts must document quarterly rebalancing whenever individual stock
appreciation pushes holding weights above the 20% Section 4.2 concentration ceiling.

4.3 HIGH-BETA & DERIVATIVE INSTRUMENTS
Aggressive portfolios may incorporate unrestricted high-beta equities (e.g. NVDA, TSLA, speculative tech),
options hedging structures, and private placement equity tranches.

========================================================
SECTION 5: ESG & SUSTAINABILITY POLICY INTERACTION
========================================================

5.1 ENVIRONMENTAL, SOCIAL, AND GOVERNANCE (ESG) CRITERIA
Apex Financial provides specialized ESG-screened portfolio overlays upon client request.
When an ESG mandate is active, investments in companies with severe fossil fuel extraction
or weapons manufacturing exposure are filtered out.

5.2 PRIORITY OF MANDATES & POLICY CONFLICT RESOLUTION
In the event of a conflict between ESG preferences and core risk rules:
The risk guidelines in Section 2, Section 3, and Section 4 ALWAYS TAKE PRECEDENCE over ESG preferences.
Specifically: An ESG mandate does NOT grant permission to override Section 4.2 aggressive trading limits,
nor does it allow exceeding the 30% equity cap for Conservative clients. Risk management is non-negotiable.

========================================================
SECTION 6: COMPLIANCE RECORDKEEPING & AUDIT
========================================================

All client advisory recommendations must be registered in the centralized advisory CRM
within 24 hours of client communication. Compliance audits are executed bi-annually.
