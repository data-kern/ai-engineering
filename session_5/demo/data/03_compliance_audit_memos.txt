SUBJECT: Forensic Audit into Unapproved High-Risk Transactions & Aggressive Trading Breaches

1. EXECUTIVE FINDINGS
A forensic scan of Q4 2025 trades revealed 14 incidents where junior advisors failed to enforce
Section 4.2 single-stock concentration ceilings in aggressive growth accounts. In three instances,
client accounts exceeded 28% concentration in high-multiple speculative semiconductor equities,
in direct violation of the 20% limit stipulated in Section 4.2.

2. TRADING FREEZES & SANCTIONED TICKERS
Compliance has initiated temporary trading restrictions on the following high-volatility instruments
pending full compliance remediation:
  - Speculative Biotech Growth Fund (Ticker: BIOX-ETF)
  - Ultra-Leveraged Clean Energy Derivative Notes (CUSIP: 99482A10)
Advisors attempting to execute orders in these tickers will receive error code ERR-COMPLIANCE-901.

3. DISCIPLINARY ACTIONS & REGULATORY ESCALATIONS
Two wealth managers have been placed on 90-day supervisory probation.
All advisor interactions regarding client risk overrides are now under active electronic surveillance.
Any external AI query attempting to surface this document without Tier 4 clearance represents
an immediate internal data breach risk.
