APEX FINANCIAL PARTNERS
INVESTMENT RISK POLICY 2026
INTERNAL DOCUMENT — NOT FOR PUBLIC DISTRIBUTION

========================================================
SECTION 1: CLIENT RISK CATEGORIES
========================================================

Apex Financial classifies every client into one of three risk categories:

  - Conservative
  - Moderate
  - Aggressive

The category is assigned at onboarding based on the client's investment
horizon, liquidity needs, income stability, and personal risk questionnaire.
No advisor may override this category without a signed review from the
Compliance team.

========================================================
SECTION 2: CONSERVATIVE CLIENT RULES
========================================================

Conservative clients prioritise capital preservation above all else.
They have a low tolerance for short-term losses.

2.1  MAXIMUM EQUITY EXPOSURE
     Conservative clients must not hold more than 30% of their total
     portfolio in equity instruments of any kind. This limit applies
     to individual stocks, equity ETFs, and equity mutual funds combined.

2.2  INDIVIDUAL STOCK CONCENTRATION
     No single stock may represent more than 5% of the total portfolio
     value for a Conservative client.

2.3  HIGH-VOLATILITY RESTRICTION
     Advisors must not recommend any individual equity with a 12-month
     beta greater than 1.5 to a Conservative client. High-beta stocks
     include but are not limited to: growth technology stocks, speculative
     biotech, leveraged ETFs, and single-country emerging market funds.

2.4  APPROVED INSTRUMENTS FOR CONSERVATIVE CLIENTS
     Preferred instruments for Conservative portfolios include:
       - Government bonds (sovereign-rated AAA or AA)
       - Investment-grade corporate bonds (BBB or higher)
       - Broad market index funds (S&P 500, FTSE 100)
       - Dividend-paying blue-chip stocks (beta < 0.8)
       - Money market funds
       - Fixed deposits

2.5  PROHIBITED ACTIONS
     The following actions are STRICTLY PROHIBITED for Conservative clients:
       - Recommending concentrated positions in growth or tech stocks
       - Suggesting margin or leveraged investments
       - Advising allocation above 30% in any equity category
       - Recommending individual stocks with beta > 1.5

========================================================
SECTION 3: MODERATE CLIENT RULES
========================================================

Moderate clients seek a balance between growth and stability. They can
tolerate moderate short-term losses in exchange for long-term appreciation.

3.1  MAXIMUM EQUITY EXPOSURE
     Moderate clients may hold up to 60% of their total portfolio in
     equity instruments.

3.2  INDIVIDUAL STOCK CONCENTRATION
     No single stock may represent more than 10% of the total portfolio.

3.3  HIGH-VOLATILITY ALLOWANCE
     Moderate clients may hold equities with a beta up to 2.0, but
     the total allocation to high-beta (beta > 1.5) stocks must not
     exceed 20% of the total portfolio.

3.4  APPROVED INSTRUMENTS FOR MODERATE CLIENTS
     Approved instruments include all Conservative instruments plus:
       - Growth stocks in established sectors (technology, healthcare)
       - Real Estate Investment Trusts (REITs)
       - Sector ETFs

========================================================
SECTION 4: AGGRESSIVE CLIENT RULES
========================================================

Aggressive clients prioritise long-term growth and can tolerate
significant short-term volatility and drawdowns.

4.1  MAXIMUM EQUITY EXPOSURE
     Aggressive clients may hold up to 90% of their total portfolio in
     equity instruments.

4.2  INDIVIDUAL STOCK CONCENTRATION
     No single stock may represent more than 20% of the total portfolio.

4.3  HIGH-VOLATILITY ALLOWANCE
     Aggressive clients may hold any publicly traded equity without
     beta restrictions, provided full risk disclosure is on file.

4.4  APPROVED INSTRUMENTS FOR AGGRESSIVE CLIENTS
     All instruments are permitted for Aggressive clients, including:
       - Individual growth and technology stocks (NVDA, TSLA, etc.)
       - Leveraged ETFs (with risk disclosure)
       - Small-cap and micro-cap stocks
       - Emerging market funds

========================================================
SECTION 5: COMPLIANCE AND ENFORCEMENT
========================================================

5.1  All client recommendations must be documented in the CRM system
     within 24 hours of the advisory session.

5.2  Any recommendation that exceeds the limits in this policy requires
     written sign-off from the Head of Compliance before being shared
     with the client.

5.3  Advisors found to have recommended instruments outside the client's
     risk category are subject to disciplinary review and may be required
     to complete additional compliance training.

5.4  This policy is reviewed annually by the Compliance Committee.
     Version: 2026.1 | Effective Date: 1 January 2026
